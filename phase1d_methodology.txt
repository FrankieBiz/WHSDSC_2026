1. PROCESS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: "How did you clean or transform the raw data before analysis?"
(~50 words)

We aggregated the 25,827 line-level records into 1,312 game-level summaries by summing goals, xG, shots, and penalties per team per game. Special-teams rows (PP, PK, empty net) were separated from even-strength play. We built a team-game panel by unstacking home/away sides, enabling rolling computations across each team's sequential game history.

---

Q: "Did you create any additional variables?"
(~25 words)

Yes: margin-adjusted ELO ratings, multi-window rolling averages (7/15/30 games), opponent-adjusted xG, Bayesian-smoothed win rates, goalie GSAx, line-level xG per 60 seconds, and home-vs-away differentials.

Q: "How did you use the selected software, and for what purposes?"
(~50 words)

Python (pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, SciPy, matplotlib) handled all data processing, feature engineering, model training, and visualization. Pandas performed game-level aggregation and rolling statistics. Scikit-learn trained and cross-validated our ensemble models. SciPy optimized blend weights via Nelder-Mead. Matplotlib generated the visualization. Excel was used only to review the matchup schedule.

---

Q: "What statistical methods did you employ?"
(~100 words)

We built a five-model stacked ensemble: Logistic Regression, two Gradient Boosting classifiers (differing in depth and tree count), Random Forest, and Extra Trees. Each generated out-of-fold predictions via 5-fold stratified cross-validation. Blend weights were optimized using Nelder-Mead minimization to maximize AUC. Feature selection used Extra Trees importance scores to reduce 208 engineered features to the top 50. ELO ratings incorporated win-margin multipliers for both goals and xG. Opponent-adjusted xG normalized each team's output by their opponent's rolling offensive strength. Bayesian smoothing with an informative prior (k=5) stabilized win rates for teams with limited data. Pearson and Spearman correlations assessed the disparity-performance relationship.

3. YOUR PREDICTIONS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q (1a): "How did you determine power rankings and matchup win probabilities?"
(~50 words)

Power rankings combine nine percentile-ranked metrics: goals-based ELO (20%), xG-based ELO (18%), 30-game xG% (15%), Bayesian win rate (12%), opponent-adjusted xG% (12%), power-play xGF (10%), goalie GSAx (7%), goalie win rate (4%), and penalty discipline (2%). Matchup probabilities come from our optimized five-model ensemble using each team's latest rolling features.

---

Q (1b): "How did you approach summarizing offensive line quality disparity?"
(~50 words)

We computed each team's first-line and second-line xG per 60 seconds of ice time, then adjusted for opponent defensive pairing strength. The adjustment normalizes by the league-average defensive xGA/60, boosting xG earned against tough defenses and penalizing production against weak ones. The disparity ratio is adjusted first-line xG/60 divided by adjusted second-line xG/60.

---

Q (1c): "What data visualization choices did you make to clarify the numbers and emphasize takeaways?"
(~50 words)

We chose a scatter plot with four-color quadrant coding to immediately convey each team's category: balanced-strong, top-heavy-strong, balanced-weak, or top-heavy-weak. Median reference lines divide the space. A regression trend line with Pearson/Spearman statistics quantifies the relationship. Team labels, quadrant shading, and a detailed caption ensure the chart is self-explanatory without additional context.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: "How did you assess your model performance?"
(~50 words)

We used 5-fold stratified cross-validation with out-of-fold (OOF) predictions to prevent data leakage. Our optimized blend achieved 0.683 AUC. We established a theoretical ceiling of ~0.69 AUC by testing whether same-game xG perfectly predicts outcomes—our model reaches 99% of this ceiling, confirming we extracted nearly all predictive signal available.
